# Grigoriev Collage: 36 Extracted Illustrations

These PNGs are native-resolution crops from the user's 2880 × 2880 `download.webp`.
No AI generation, repainting, sharpening, or upscaling was used. Zooming enlarges
the supplied pixels; it does not recover lost detail. Some compositions were already
cropped in the supplied collage. Plate 24 is a partial view of a larger illustration.

subjects**. Probable labels are editorial inferences, not catalogue titles. Numbers

## Research, 18 September 2026 (atlas-isd0)

Every published list of titles that could be found was checked against the
unidentified and probable plates: the two Tretyakov Gallery Magazine articles (T21,
T23), the museum-supplied selection in English and Russian (GW), the Sotheby's 2007
lot, and press on the Fabergé Museum exhibition (Kommersant, St Petersburg
Vedomosti). Together they title about 25 of the 58 sheets; **none of the fifteen
plates below is among them**, so none gets a character or chapter link. What follows
are readings to test, not identifications.

| Plate | Candidate reading | For | Against |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 8 | Zossima and Alyosha walking in the monastery | Same faces and dress as plate 1; the elder's halo | No such walk occurs in the text |
| 11 | The family in the elder's cell (II.2) | Men at a table, a figure in a robe | The cell has no wine or books; Grigoriev paints those scenes with monks in black (plates 15, 28) |
| 21 | Alyosha falls to the earth (VII.4, "Cana of Galilee") | A prone figure at night; follows plate 3, Alyosha at the coffin | The text has stars, the cathedral and flower beds, not a birch wood; the crop may hold a second figure |
| 31 | Samsonov and his son (VIII.1) | Old man in a kaftan and beard, son in European dress, arched shapes that may be pier mirrors | The son is "closely-shaven"; the room is vast in the text |
| 32 | Plotnikov's grocery (VIII.5) | The one shop the novel describes selling "tea, coffee, sugar"; evening | The text calls it the largest shop in town; no Mitya, no troika |

Plate 31 was relabelled: the standing figure, enlarged, reads as a man, so "a bearded
man and a woman" was withdrawn. AI-enhanced copies of plates 11, 21, 31 and 32 were
also examined and rejected as evidence: each invents detail (see
`src/assets/studies/README.md`).

**The next step is not more searching online.** The Fabergé Museum published the whole
cycle as an album in 2026; its list of plates would settle most of these.

Source identifications were matched visually to the published reproductions.
English names follow the app's Garnett spelling. Chapter links in the app are
editorial reading companions, not claimed museum catalogue metadata.

No identity is assigned to individual jury members. “Katya the schoolgirl” is not
linked to Katerina merely because their names resemble each other. Plate 18 is not
labeled Lise. Plate 34 is not the published *Ivan in his father's house*. These are
intentional limits, not missing labels.
