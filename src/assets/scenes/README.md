# Original Scene Illustrations

Six JPEGs supplied by the user on 17 September 2026, copied without alteration.
These are reproductions of Boris Grigoriev's historical illustrations, **not
generated studies**. The original filenames correspond to the illustration sequence
in Dmitry Fomin's [“An Amazing Mixture of Good and Evil”](https://www.tretyakovgallerymagazine.com/articles/4-2021-73/amazing-mixture-good-and-evil),
The Tretyakov Gallery Magazine, issue 4/2021 (73).

The magazine captions date the works 1916–1932 and credit a private collection in
the USA, reproduced from Sotheby's *Russian Art*, volume II, New York, 17 April
2007. The article discusses differing dates for the cycle; this project uses the
caption dates, not a claim that scholarship has settled that question. User supply
is the provenance for these repository files, not an assertion of an open license.

Scene summaries are editorial reminders checked against the local Garnett text.
The Snegiryov illustration names the people rather than an exact chapter; IV.7 is
the editorial reading companion for their outdoor conversation. The confession
link opens III.3; the page explicitly notes its continuation through III.5.

The homepage keeps the complete compositions beside their descriptions. Next.js
serves responsive derivatives, with lazy loading below the first spread. Each scene
links to the source, its reading chapter, and indexed participants. Marya
Kondratyevna is named in the guitar scene but has no separate character route.
