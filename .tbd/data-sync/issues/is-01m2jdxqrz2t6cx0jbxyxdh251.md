The edition's title page runs: title / 'A NOVEL IN FOUR PARTS AND AN EPILOGUE BY' / author / 'FROM THE RUSSIAN BY CONSTANCE GARNETT' / device / LONDON / WILLIAM HEINEMANN / 1912.

The plates borrow those conventions but no page is actually laid out as a title page. The overview is where the idiom belongs most — it is the first leaf.

Use an original device; do not trace a publisher's mark.
