The app is set as the 1912 Heinemann edition of the Garnett translation — the text the corpus comes from. Letterpress ink on cream stock, letterspaced capitals, paired rules, centred blocks.

Specification: docs/design-system.md. Working checklist: .claude/skills/first-edition/SKILL.md, mirrored to .agents/skills/ so Codex picks it up.

Governing rule: the page is a leaf of stock, not a screen. A boxed card, a shadow, a gradient or a rounded panel is wrong by definition.

Supersedes the blue/teal (v2) and four-hue (v3) screen palettes.
