A documented pre-reading barrier: readers must choose between Pevear & Volokhonsky, Garnett, McDuff and Avsey with no basis for choosing, and the Goodreads thread on it runs for dozens of replies.

An honest page on what each does — P&V closest to the Russian at some cost in fluency, Garnett's Victorian gravity suiting Zossima, McDuff leaner for a first read, Avsey's dialogue the most alive.

This must include what it costs us: we ship Garnett because it is public domain, and the standing critique of Garnett is that she smooths the rough edges and individuates the voices less. For a novel whose entire method is that every idea is spoken by a particular person, that is a real limitation, and hiding it would be dishonest.
