The payoff. Having recorded verdicts against reading position, show the reader their own trajectory: where the legal answer and the moral answer came apart, when they changed their mind, and what they had just read when it happened.

Then set it against two fixed reference lines — the jury's verdict (all of it on Dmitri) and Zossima's claim (all of us, all of it). The reader's own line sits somewhere between, and where it sits is the point.

This is the 'oh my god' moment if it lands: not admiring a diagram, but being shown the exact chapter where you quietly stopped agreeing with the court.
