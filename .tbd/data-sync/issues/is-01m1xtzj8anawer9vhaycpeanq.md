Nothing in this novel is asserted by the narrator; every idea is spoken by someone who has something riding on it. Ivan formulates that all things are lawful, Smerdyakov acts on it, Zossima answers with universal responsibility, Alyosha lives it.

A view that presents the argument as what it is — a debate with named parties — and lets the reader follow one position through the book, with the passages where it is stated, contested, and tested.

Depends on evidence records for the relation data.
